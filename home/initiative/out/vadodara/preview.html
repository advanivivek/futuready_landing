What attendees say

FAQs

Who can attend?

College students from any year and any college in and around Vadodara, from first-year beginners to final-year students, especially CSE, IT, AI and data science, and allied branches. Seniors get harder challenges in the same room.

Do I need to pay?

No. Futuready is free for students.

Where exactly is it, and at what time?

At Parul University, Vadodara. We will email you the hall and the time before the day, and they will be on this page too.

Do I need a laptop?

No. Your phone is enough for the whole session. For the build, one laptop per squad of four helps, but it is not a must.

Do I need to know AI already?

No. If you have used any AI chatbot even once, you are ready. If you have used none, you will leave having built something with one.

What should I bring?

A charged phone and your curiosity. A laptop if you have one.

Why register?

It helps us plan seats and saves you time at the door. Registering is not a guarantee of a seat; entry is confirmed on the day.

How do I bring Futuready to my college?

Ask your Training and Placement cell or your tech club to write to support@hack2skill.com.

See you at Futuready.
