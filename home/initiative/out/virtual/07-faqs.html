Who can attend?

College students from any year and any college, anywhere in India, from first-year beginners to final-year students. If you can join a video call, you can join Futuready.

Do I need to pay?

No. Futuready is free for students.

How do I join a session?

Register once on this page. Before every session we email you the date, the time and the joining link, so you can come to the next one or any later one.

Do I need a laptop?

A phone is enough to play along. A laptop helps for the build in the boss level, but it is not a must.

Do I need to know AI already?

No. If you have used any AI chatbot even once, you are ready. If you have used none, you will leave having built something with one.

What should I bring?

A charged phone with internet, a quiet corner, and your curiosity. A laptop if you have one.

Why register?

Registering once keeps you on the list for every session: you get each date and joining link by email.

How do I bring Futuready to my college?

Ask your Training and Placement cell or your tech club to write to support@hack2skill.com.
