Futuready Builders Lab

AI now writes most of the code. It finds its own bugs, fixes them and even tests its own security. So what will make you valuable in 2031?

Futuready Builders Lab is a series of 2-hour, hands-on bootcamps at zero cost that answers that question on your campus. No long lectures. Phones out, squads up, learn and build for the future.

Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

Who can attend?

College students from any year, from first-year beginners to final-year students, especially CSE, IT, AI and data science, and allied branches. Seniors get harder challenges in the same room.

Do I need to pay?

No. Futuready Builders Lab is at zero cost for students.

Do I need a laptop?

No. Your phone is enough for the whole session. For the build, one laptop per squad of four helps, but it is not a must.

Do I need to know AI already?

No. If you have used any AI chatbot even once, you are ready. If you have used none, you will leave having built something with one.

What should I bring?

A charged phone and your curiosity. A laptop if you have one.

Why pre-register?

It helps your college plan seats and it saves you time at the door. Pre-registering is not a guarantee of a seat; your college confirms entry on the day.

How do I bring Futuready to my college?

Ask your Training and Placement cell or your tech club to write to support@hack2skill.com. You can also choose "Bring Futuready to my college" in the form.
